Space infrastructure cannot scale if every structure must arrive complete from Earth.

Launch dependence, human construction limits and communication delay create an industrial bottleneck. The next leap is not only reaching space — it is building, repairing and manufacturing there.

05 / HOW ASCS WORKS ANOMALY-READY CONSTRUCTION

Plan. Position.
Print. Assemble.

The robot fleet follows a pre-installed construction plan, then uses AI recovery procedures and ground supervision when reality differs from the model.

  1. PlanFactory blueprint + robotic sequence
  2. PositionNavigation, docking and alignment
  3. PrintAdditive manufacturing / feedstock use
  4. AssembleRobotic joining and installation
  5. InspectVision, sensors and metrology
  6. CorrectAI re-plan or ground-control action
