WHO WE ARE

Research first.

VINASTRA Aerospace explores autonomous robotics, AI, additive manufacturing, and the systems thinking required for future space industry.

WHAT WE ARE DEVELOPING

ASCS, the construction layer.

The proposed Autonomous Space Construction System combines an AI mission planner, digital twin, robotic builders, machine vision, and ground control.

WHERE WE STAND

Concept to demonstrator.

Current work is centered on concept design, feasibility studies, CAD models, simulations, and a planned ground-based ASCS demonstrator. No deployed space factory or operational robot is claimed.
