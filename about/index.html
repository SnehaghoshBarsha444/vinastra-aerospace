01 / PHILOSOPHY

Research-Driven Pragmatism

We do not treat space industrialization as speculative fiction. Vinastra advances through mathematically sound engineering: rigorous kinematics modeling, closed-loop machine vision, high-fidelity physics simulations, and empirical ground-demonstrator validation.

02 / FOUNDERSHIP

Founder & Vision

Founded and led by Vinay Jeedi, Vinastra Aerospace is built on the conviction that Indian DeepTech engineering talent can establish world-class intellectual property in space robotics, automated assembly, and in-situ manufacturing infrastructure.

03 / DISCLOSURE

Strict Intellectual Honesty

We maintain transparent disclosure: Vinastra does not claim operational space factories, orbiting robotic hardware, commercial customer contracts, or secured venture rounds. We are in active concept architecture, software modeling, and prototype planning.

04 / THE CORE THESIS

Launch The Builders

Do not attempt to launch monolithic space factories from Earth's deep gravity well. Instead, launch compact, intelligent, autonomous robotic builder cells capable of assembling, joining, and inspecting modular structures directly in microgravity.

05 / TALENT & ECOSYSTEM

Indian DeepTech Hub

Anchored in India's premier aerospace and software corridors (Hyderabad and Bengaluru), Vinastra is building a multidisciplinary engineering team bridging robotics, computer vision, embedded firmware, and orbital mechanics.

06 / NEAR-TERM TARGET

Prototype-1 Demonstrator

Our 18-month primary technical milestone is the ground-based Prototype-1: proving the end-to-end loop of CAD translation, robotic positioning, additive joining, and vision-based fault recovery in an Earth-based laboratory setting.
