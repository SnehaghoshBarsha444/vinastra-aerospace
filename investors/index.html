01 / MACRO TAILWINDS · THE $1.8T SPACE HORIZON

The transition from launch capability to in-space industry.

Reusable launch vehicles have compressed launch costs by over 90%. As access to orbit becomes commoditized, enterprise value shifts to what can be built, serviced, and manufactured in microgravity:

MARKET SIGNAL 01

$630B to $1.8 Trillion Expansion

According to the World Economic Forum and McKinsey ("The $1.8 Trillion Opportunity for Global Economic Growth"), the global space economy is projected to reach $1.8 Trillion by 2035, driven by communications, in-orbit servicing, and space-based manufacturing.

MARKET SIGNAL 02

The ISAM Market Emergence

In-Space Servicing, Assembly, and Manufacturing (ISAM) is a critical national security and commercial priority for NASA, ESA, ISRO, and private constellation operators looking to extend satellite lifetimes and build large apertures.

MARKET SIGNAL 03

India's SpaceTech Momentum

Supported by progressive IN-SPACe policies, India's private SpaceTech ecosystem offers world-class robotics, software, and systems engineering at a fraction of Western development capital, generating exceptional capital efficiency.
