03 / Prototype & roadmap

From concept
to demonstrator.

The near-term technical objective is a ground-based ASCS demonstrator. The stages below describe planned work, not completed results.

PROTOTYPE-1GROUND
DEMONSTRATOR● PLANNED OBJECTIVE
PROTOTYPE-1 / PLANNED

Autonomous assembly.

Robotic systems would read a digital blueprint, position and join modular structural elements, and operate under ground-control supervision.

PROTOTYPE-1 / PLANNED

Print, inspect, compare.

The demonstrator is intended to combine robotic additive manufacturing, machine-vision inspection, digital-twin comparison, and ground monitoring.

PROTOTYPE-1 / PLANNED

Detect and recover.

Fault detection and recovery workflows would test misalignment response and corrective actions before any space mission is attempted.

PHASE 1 / PRESENT

Concept and feasibility.

Concept design, feasibility studies, AI robotics R&D, CAD models, and simulations.

PHASE 2 / GROUND PROTOTYPES

Build the systems.

Robotic assembly, autonomous inspection, 3D printing, and control software.

PHASE 3 / TECHNOLOGY VALIDATION

Integrate and test.

Integrated demonstrations, environmental testing, and potential orbital experiments.

PHASE 4 / INITIAL SPACE INFRASTRUCTURE

Demonstrate in space.

Demonstrate robotic assembly and small-scale manufacturing in space.

PHASE 5 / INDUSTRIAL EXPANSION

Expand the platform.

Explore resource processing, larger factories, servicing, and logistics.

PHASE 6 / DEEP-SPACE FACTORY

Pursue the long horizon.

Scalable manufacturing and autonomous industrial systems beyond Earth.

DECK TIMELINE / INDICATIVE

2026–2030+.

The investor deck presents architecture and team work in 2026, a ground demo in 2027, an integrated cell in 2028, relevant-environment testing in 2029, and a potential space demo from 2030+. These are proposed milestones, not guaranteed dates.
