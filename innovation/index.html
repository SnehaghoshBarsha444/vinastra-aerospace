Build beyond

CORE THESISASCS● PROPOSED PLATFORM
01 / LAUNCH DEPENDENCE

Mass, volume, cost.

Large structures are constrained by payload mass, fairing volume, and launch cost. Additive construction could use feedstock to create useful parts and structures in space.

02 / HUMAN CONSTRUCTION LIMITS

People cannot do every task.

Deep-space construction, inspection, and repair cannot rely on astronauts for every operation. Robotic builders are intended to reduce dependence on human EVA.

03 / COMMUNICATION DELAY

Autonomy at distance.

Deep-space delays prevent real-time manual control. ASCS is proposed as an autonomous-first system with human supervision and remote recovery.

04 / ASCS SUBSYSTEMS

Plan, build, verify.

AI Mission Planner, Digital Twin, Robotic Builders, Machine Vision, and Ground Control form the intended construction layer.

05 / FACTORY CONCEPT

Modular and expandable.

The proposed factory could support in-space manufacturing, servicing, logistics, materials science, refining, and mission support for lunar, Martian, and deep-space activity.

06 / RESOURCE ECONOMY / FUTURE CONCEPT

From asteroid to feedstock.

Asteroid resources could move through robotic mining, transport, purification, refining, and additive manufacturing. VINASTRA does not currently operate these systems.
